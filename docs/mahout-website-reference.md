# Mahout Website Reference

Source: `Mahout_Website_Master_Brief_V2_Deep_Detailed.pdf`

Purpose: a concise local reference for future website passes. This file summarizes the PDF only; it is not a full dump and should not be treated as permission to invent new product claims, pricing, features, assets, or routing.

## 1. Website North Star / Brand Spine

Final brand line:

> Five elements. One North Star. A living system for becoming who you said you wanted to be.

The website must make people remember:

- Mountain shows where you are going.
- Path shows what you are doing now.
- Elephant shows how you are carrying it.
- Mahout helps you understand it.
- Brain remembers and learns.
- North Star helps you become it.

Mahout is not a generic productivity app. It is an AI life operating system that connects future vision, goals, actions, emotions, reflections, habits, behaviors, trends, patterns, memory, letters, reviews, and guidance into one living system.

Promise hierarchy:

1. Mahout is not just a task app, habit tracker, journal, mood tracker, or chatbot.
2. Mahout connects those pieces into one calm life system.
3. The user begins with a future vision.
4. That future vision forms North Star: the ideal future self / guide.
5. Mountain turns vision into Future Goals and Today Goals.
6. Path turns goals into current actions, timers, checkoffs, and receipts.
7. Elephant adds emotional context.
8. Mahout adds reflection and meaning.
9. The Brain remembers and learns.
10. North Star uses the loop to guide with more context over time.

## 2. Surgical Editing Rules

- Inspect the newest repo state first. If the live site already has part of a section, improve it surgically instead of replacing the whole site.
- Preserve the story: Mahout is a five-element life system centered on a North Star formed from the user’s future vision.
- Treat copy as product logic. Do not invent features, prices, privacy/security claims, unlimited usage, or unsupported subscription tiers.
- Build story-first. Explain why Mahout exists before listing features.
- Use assets as proof. Symbolic element illustrations explain the world; screenshots prove the product is real.
- Avoid repetition. Each section should add one new layer of understanding.
- Patch the smallest missing section. Do not duplicate existing sections.
- Keep a component map: Hero, Story, FiveElements, ConnectedLoop, Brain, HabitsBehaviors, LettersReviews, Modes, Pricing, FAQ.
- Do not change global routing, package structure, build tooling, or styling framework unless explicitly requested.
- Assets are separate from content. Use placeholders only where assets are missing and document the exact slot and filename.
- Mobile and responsive polish come before desktop gimmicks.
- Use optimized images, lazy loading, lightweight animations, and alt text.

## 3. Core Story: Five Elements. One North Star.

Mahout connects the user’s future vision, goals, actions, emotions, reflections, and guidance into one calm system.

The story starts with the life the user is trying to build. From that vision, Mahout forms the North Star: the ideal future version of the user, able to guide the present self back toward what matters.

The five elements work together:

- Mountain holds goals and direction.
- Path tracks actions happening right now.
- Elephant helps the user understand emotional weather.
- Mahout gives space to reflect.
- North Star connects everything and guides forward.

Key contrast:

- A task app tracks actions.
- A habit app tracks streaks.
- A journal stores reflections.
- A mood app tracks emotions.
- A chatbot answers questions.
- Mahout connects them.

Hero copy source options:

- “Build a life with a North Star.”

## 4. North Star Origin Story

North Star begins with the user’s future life.

Mahout asks the user to imagine the version of life where the important things worked out: work, health, relationships, freedom, identity, peace, and impact. From that vision, Mahout helps shape the North Star: the ideal future version of the user that can speak back, remind them what matters, and guide present-day decisions.

Rules for explaining North Star:

- Say North Star is the user’s ideal future self, formed from their future life vision.
- Say North Star becomes more useful because it connects to goals, actions, moods, reflection, memory, and patterns.
- Do not say North Star magically knows everything.
- Do not say North Star guarantees success or fixes life.
- Do not say North Star is therapy or mental health treatment.

Needed proof slot:

- Onboarding / future-vision screenshot for the website section “Start with the life you are building.”
- Use clean demo data such as: “Build a calm, focused, financially stable life with meaningful work, health, and presence.”

## 5. Connected System Loop

The five elements are not separate tabs; they are a loop:

1. Vision becomes North Star.
2. North Star gives direction.
3. Mountain turns direction into Future Goals and Today Goals.
4. Path turns goals into actions right now.
5. Repeated Path actions become habits after 21 consistent days.
6. Longer consistency can reveal behaviors after 66 days.
7. Elephant adds emotional context.
8. Mahout adds reflection and meaning.
9. Brain stores memory, trends, and patterns.
10. North Star uses all of it to write letters, reviews, and guidance.

Loop language:

- Future vision / North Star: “Your ideal future self begins as a vision of the life you are building.”
- Direction / Mountain: “Mountain holds Future Goals and Today Goals so the day stays connected to direction.”
- Action now / Path: “Path is what you are doing right now.”
- Repetition / Habits and Behaviors: “Habits show what you repeat; behaviors show what repetition is becoming.”
- Emotion / Elephant: “Your emotions are not noise; they are part of the map.”
- Meaning / Mahout: “Reflection turns data into meaning.”
- Memory and intelligence / Brain: “Brain keeps North Star from being generic.”
- Guidance / North Star: “North Star guides present self toward future self.”

## 6. Five Elements Definitions

### Mountain

Mountain holds direction: Future Goals, Today Goals, deadlines, reviews, and the few lanes that deserve attention today. It keeps the day from becoming a scattered list.

Show:

- Future Goals
- Today Goals
- A-E priority lanes / overflow where applicable
- Deadlines
- Goal reviews
- Linking goals to Path actions

Memorable line: “Mountain shows where you are going.”

Recommended visual: a serene abstract mountain with glowing lane markers, a small path up the slope, and a North Star above the peak.

### Path

Path is where goals become action right now. It holds concrete steps, timers, checkoffs, reminders, Path sessions/time, linked goal actions, and receipts of effort.

Show:

- Actions
- Timers
- Checkoffs
- Reminders
- Path sessions/time
- Linked goal actions
- Receipts of effort

Memorable line: “Path carries the next honest step.”

Recommended visual: a clean glowing path made of stepping stones, timer rings, and checkmarks leading from a mountain toward the viewer.

### Elephant

Elephant helps the user notice what they are carrying. Mood logs and emotional check-ins help North Star understand the weather behind actions.

Show:

- Mood logs
- Emotional check-ins
- Mood trends
- Feeling-state context for guidance
- Personal rhythm

Memorable line: “Your emotions are not noise. They are part of the map.”

Recommended visual: an elegant minimal elephant silhouette surrounded by weather symbols: moon, cloud, rain, sun, and soft rhythm waves.

### Mahout

Mahout is the reflective space where the user slows down and tells the truth. Journaling and reflection turn the day into meaning, not just completion.

Show:

- Journal entries
- Reflections
- Saved insights
- Meaning
- Identity
- Self-understanding
- Companion guidance if visible

Memorable line: “Some days do not need more pressure. They need to be understood.”

Recommended visual: an open journal with glowing handwritten lines, an inner compass, soft memory fragments, and lavender shadows.

### North Star

North Star is the user’s ideal future self: the version shaped by the life they deeply want to build. It guides with context: goals, actions, moods, reflections, patterns, memories, and receipts.

Show:

- Live guidance
- Morning and Evening Letters
- Weekly Reviews
- Brain / memory continuity
- Modes such as Auto, Analytic, Reflective, Motivator, Mentor
- North Star Credits for live / deeper on-demand intelligence

Memorable line: “North Star is not just AI. It is guidance with memory, direction, and receipts.”

Recommended visual: a glowing star / compass point with a faint future-self silhouette and thin lines connected to goals, actions, moods, reflection, and memory.

## 9. Visual System Rules

Mahout should feel premium spiritual-operational: calm, intelligent, emotionally grounded, and deeply usable. It should not look like a generic SaaS page or therapy / AI gimmick.

Use:

- Palette: ivory/warm white, soft lavender, royal purple, midnight violet, muted gold, soft sage, blue-gray.
- Shapes: rounded cards, soft rings, constellation lines, glass surfaces, subtle shadows.
- Typography: large calm hero headlines, compact explanatory body, section badges, generous but not empty spacing.
- Illustrations: transparent 3D/glass symbolic assets with consistent lighting.
- Screenshots: clean phone mockups with carefully staged demo data.
- Motion: slow glow, orbit, soft fade, gentle loop diagrams.
- Interaction: anchor links, “Explore the five elements,” section cards that expand or scroll smoothly.

Avoid:

- Neon, crypto gold, harsh red/orange, dark aggressive marketplace style.
- Sharp corporate icons, noisy dashboards, childish cartoons.
- Tiny feature text, overlong paragraphs, all-caps shouting.
- Random stock photos or inconsistent AI art styles.
- Raw screenshots with debug labels, clipped text, personal data, or old UI.
- Bouncy gamified animation or flashy AI effects.
- Overcomplicated scroll hijacking or animations that hide content.

Layout rhythm:

- Hero must be memorable, not crowded.
- Every major section needs one sentence, one visual, and one proof point.
- Alternate emotional story sections with product proof screenshots.
- Use white/lavender space intentionally; avoid huge empty cards.
- Do not overuse tables. Use card groups and diagrams for value; keep detailed comparison lower.

## 10. Forbidden Claims

Do not claim:

- North Star magically knows everything.
- North Star guarantees success or fixes life.
- Mahout is therapy, medical care, mental health treatment, or crisis support.
- Unsupported privacy/security claims.
- Unlimited usage.
- Clinical outcomes.
- Encryption unless verified.
- Real-person mentor impersonation.
- “Tokens” for North Star Credits.
- Top-ups.
- Plus.
- Weekly plan.
- Family plan.
- Lifetime plan.
- Remove limits.
- Unlock premium features.
- Limited-time discount unless true and legally supported.

Mentor mode must use language like lens, influences, standards, questions, and perspectives. It must not claim to impersonate real people.

Habits and behaviors must be explained without overclaiming science. Use the PDF’s wording: after 21 consistent successes, repeated action can begin to look like a Habit; after 66 consistent successes, deeper Behavior patterns can start to emerge.

## 11. Free vs Pro / North Star Credits Truth

Free is useful. Pro deepens the intelligence layer.

Free:

- Daily foundation remains useful: Mountain, Path, Elephant, and Mahout basics.
- Starter North Star Credits.
- Basic/core continuity and readable saved context.
- Preview / focused access for trends and patterns.
- Focused preview set for habits and behaviors.
- Limited letters and reviews where product allows.
- Foundation and starter guidance.

Pro:

- Same foundation, deeper North Star guidance.
- More monthly North Star Credits.
- Deeper Brain continuity and intelligence surfaces.
- Full trends and patterns.
- Fuller habits and behaviors command center.
- Morning/evening letters and weekly reviews.
- Richer, more contextual guidance.

Pricing copy from PDF:

- Pro Monthly — $7.99 / month — Flexible.
- Pro Yearly Founder — $54.99 / year — Only $4.58 / month — Best value — Save 43% vs monthly.

North Star Credits trust block:

- North Star Credits are used for live North Star replies and deeper on-demand intelligence.
- They are not used for Path actions, Mountain goals, Elephant mood logs, Mahout journaling, reading saved memories, or existing letters.
- Pro refills monthly.
- Free starter credits are one-time.
